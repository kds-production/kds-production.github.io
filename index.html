LOAD BEARING

THE SYSTEMS THE WORLD QUIETLY RESTS ON

LOAD BEARING is a YouTube channel that explains the infrastructure everyday life depends on: power grids, undersea internet cables, container shipping, satellite navigation and more. Each episode is researched from public sources, which are listed in the video description.

Watch the channel: youtube.com/@loadbearingfiles

LoadBearing Uploader

LoadBearing Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the LOAD BEARING channel. It uploads videos that the owner has produced to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and translated metadata. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

Privacy Policy

LoadBearing Uploader · Last updated: 16 October 2026

LoadBearing Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the LOAD BEARING YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Who can use the app

The app is used only by the channel owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.

Data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to manage videos on the owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. With that permission it uploads videos, thumbnails and captions and sets video metadata such as titles, descriptions and translations. It may read basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

The app does not access Gmail, contacts, files in Google Drive or any other Google data, and it does not access other people's accounts.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to perform the uploads and updates the owner requests. The OAuth access token is stored privately in the owner's own working environment and is never published or shared. The app does not sell data, does not use it for advertising and does not transfer it to third parties.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or no longer needed.
